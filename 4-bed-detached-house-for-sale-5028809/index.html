Colleran Auctioneers are delighted to present 30 Grattan Park, Salthill, an exceptionally well maintained and extensively upgraded four bedroom detached family home in one of Galway`s most established and sought after residential locations.

Extending to approximately 156 sq m (1,686 sq ft), this bright and spacious home enjoys a wonderful position in a quiet cul-de-sac, only metres from a large green area and just off Salthill Promenade. Grattan Beach is on the doorstep, while Galway city centre is only a ten minute stroll along the seafront.

The accommodation is particularly well suited to family living, with a spacious sitting room, separate dining room, well equipped kitchen, utility with guest toilet and a valuable home office/study. Upstairs there are four bedrooms, including a main bedroom with ensuite, and a family bathroom. Mature front and rear gardens provide excellent outdoor space and privacy, with off-street parking to the front and additional storage buildings to the rear.

Number 30 has also benefited from considerable investment in comfort and energy efficiency, including 14 solar panels with a 10kWh battery, EV charger, cavity wall and underfloor insulation and zoned heating.

Combining generous family accommodation, impressive upgrades and a quiet setting within walking distance of both Salthill and Galway city centre, 30 Grattan Park offers a rare opportunity to acquire a quality detached home in this highly desirable location.
